Why We Selected K=4

We used the Elbow Method to test different numbers of clusters and selected K=4 as a practical choice for customer segmentation.

The four clusters represent customers based on tenure and monthly charges. This helps us compare customer behaviour, understand churn rates and identify customers who may be more likely to leave.

The Elbow Method was used to compare different numbers of clusters and examine how the within-cluster variation changed. K = 4 was chosen as a practical balance between grouping similar customers and keeping the four customer segments easy to understand and interpret.
